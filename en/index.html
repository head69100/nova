What NOVA does

Everything a console does.
And what it doesn’t.

One library for all your games

NOVA finds your installed games on its own, fetches covers, backgrounds, logos and trailers, adds new games and removes the ones you uninstall.

Built for controllers

DualSense, DualShock 4 or Xbox. Battery level, rumble, a light bar that matches the game, and a virtual Xbox controller for picky games.

On your living-room TV

One shortcut turns on the TV and shows NOVA on it (Sunshine + Moonlight). Mid-game, switch from the PC screen to the TV without quitting.

The whole family

Profiles with PIN codes, parental controls, separate saves per profile, night mode, and a player 2 tracked when two controllers play.

Your phone joins in

Scan a QR code and it becomes a second screen (temperatures, screenshot, pause) — or a friend’s controller, with nothing to install.

Stuck? NOVA Vision

A screenshot of the game, and an AI running on your PC whispers a hint without spoiling everything. Free, no account, nothing leaves your home.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is NOVA really free?

Yes, with no ads and no subscription. If you want to support the project, there’s a Sponsor button on the GitHub page, but it’s never required.

Does it replace Steam or Epic?

No: NOVA brings your games together and launches them through their usual launchers. Your purchases, achievements and cloud saves stay with Steam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft or EA.

Which controllers work?

DualSense (PS5), DualShock 4 (PS4) and Xbox controllers, over USB or Bluetooth. NOVA can also create a virtual Xbox controller for games that don’t recognize the DualSense. Keyboard and mouse work everywhere.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The installer isn’t digitally signed yet, which is expensive for a free project. Click “More info” then “Run anyway”.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. NOVA runs on your PC. It only downloads game covers and trailers, and checks GitHub for updates. The NOVA Vision AI runs on your PC too.

Can I play on my TV?

Yes, with Sunshine on the PC and the Moonlight app on the TV (Android TV, Google TV…). NOVA installs and configures Sunshine for you, then a controller shortcut turns on the TV and shows NOVA on it.
